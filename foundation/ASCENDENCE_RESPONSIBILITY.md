# Working principle: responsibility under Ascendence and Transcendence

Identifier: `WP-ASCENDENCE-01`

This is a working principle referenced from the Rule 1 document package. It is not part of the RULE-1 canonical text or digest. It is not a Research Plan amendment. Any mapping onto the existing phase axis (Ascendence → Transcendence) is an interpretation only.

Ascendence without responsibility becomes uncontrolled power. Transcendence through responsibility becomes transformative stewardship.

You don't transcend responsibility; you transcend the limitations that prevent you from fulfilling responsibility better.

## Enforcement

A tool record or knowledge record whose capability set grows must not have its authorization or permissions grow in the same change, unless a cited human decision record is present and the Rule 1 assessment is re-done. A capability increase alone never raises authorization.
