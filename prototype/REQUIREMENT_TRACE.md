# Requirement trace

Identifiers are `ULC-<tablet>-<clause>`. They are not the collection-level `UNI-001`…`UNI-015` map, which is a different register and is not implemented here.

A line that names a test means the test exercises the behaviour. It does not mean the requirement is accepted, reviewed, or complete. Thirty-one identifiers are not implemented out of 66. P4 has no close list. P5 closes only ULC-21-07. P6 closes only ULC-21-01, ULC-21-03, and ULC-21-09. P7 closes only ULC-00-11, ULC-00-12 (partial), ULC-16-05, ULC-16-11, ULC-26-03, and ULC-26-05.

- ULC-01-05 — A bound derived under a premise is refused as a trajectory guarantee when the declared domain is escaped (`DomainNotForwardInvariant`) or the per-step premise has failed, including where the inequality `error <= bound` still holds. Within a failing step the refusal order is domain, then premise, then bound. The premise may be stated by the caller or computed by the module as `residual <= epsilon`; if both are supplied and disagree, construction is refused rather than one being preferred. A step refuses a negative, NaN, or infinite magnitude, a bool where a number is required, a negative or bool step index, and a non-bool `inside_domain`/`premise_holds`. A returned `TrajectoryGuarantee` carries the number of steps it covers, the premise basis (`caller_asserted`, `computed_from_residual_and_epsilon`, `computed_and_asserted`, or `mixed`), and — when `require_forward_invariance=False` — a `forward_invariance_enforced` flag, a named waiver, and the waiver text in `limitations`, so a waived result cannot be mistaken for a full one. Attainment of the bound counts only against a strictly positive bound, so a trivially satisfied trajectory is not reported as sharp. Exercised by `test_ulc_01_05_forward_invariance.py` by `test_w1_k01_trajectory_inputs.py` (Wave 1 remediation K01/N10/N11: `require_forward_invariance` must be a real bool; `forward_invariance_report` refuses a string, scalars, non-numeric, non-finite, empty or mixed-dimension points and a predicate that does not return a bool, and its `points_checked` counts only points the predicate evaluated, with `points_supplied` reported separately), and by `test_sim003_artifacts.py`, which drives the module from the byte-identical SIM-003 record and raw trajectory of the UNI-RP-001 research package and asserts the premise breaks at step 1 while the bound is first violated at step 2. Scope: the step facts are caller-supplied and the module cannot detect a caller that supplies wrong or dishonest facts; a guarantee is a statement about the facts as supplied, not an independently established property of the modelled system. It checks a supplied trajectory only, does not prove a domain is forward invariant in general, issues no authorization, awards no credit, and is not independent verification.
